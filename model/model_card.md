**One AI. Every possibility.**

## Core Capabilities

- 🧠 **Advanced Reasoning** — complex problems, mathematics, logic
- 💻 **Coding** — Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, C++, Java, SQL, etc.
- 👁️ **Vision** — understands images, screenshots, diagrams and documents
- 🎨 **Image Generation** — creates concepts, UI designs, posters and artwork
- 📄 **Document Intelligence** — PDF, DOCX, spreadsheets and research
- 🌐 **Web Intelligence** — current information and research
- 🔧 **Tool Use** — APIs, databases, GitHub, terminals and automation
- 🎙️ **Voice Mode** — natural real-time conversation
- 🧩 **Agent Mode** — performs multi-step tasks autonomously
- 🔐 **Privacy Mode** — minimizes unnecessary data retention
